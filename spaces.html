Spaces

Two floors, 300 sqm in total, planned around one question: where will a student actually want to sit?

Six spots, one building

Main café

Purple LED accents, warm lighting, a full coffee bar and wooden tables. This is where friends meet and groups work on projects.

Study zone

Charging outlets at every seat, Wi-Fi, quiet tables and comfortable chairs. A separate quiet room sits next to it.

JDM corner

A brick wall, a neon sign and a framed car poster. It doubles as our favorite photo backdrop.

Rev Collection

Rotating diecast cars and collectibles, lit like a showroom and free to look at.

Rev Lounge

Relaxed seating for friends and classmates, with soft light and room to stretch out.

Outdoor seating

A covered terrace with tables and planters, for coffee between classes.

Rev Café is a student concept for a campus café, made for our HTML5 final project.
