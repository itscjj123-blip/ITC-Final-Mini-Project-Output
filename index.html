Rev Café

Fuel Your Study. Drive Your Passion.

Rev Café is a two-storey campus café built like a Japanese street-racing garage. Come for a good, affordable drink, stay for the study tables, and talk cars with people who care about them as much as you do.

What you will find inside

Drinks that fit a student budget

Drinks from ₱79, with Spanish, iced and matcha lattes close behind.

Places to study

Long tables with outlets, fast Wi-Fi and a quiet room upstairs.

A JDM display corner

Toy cars, car posters and a neon sign, rotated by our own members.

Events every week

Gaming nights, car meets and talks open to every student organization.

Built for long afternoons

Seventy percent of Rev Café is simply a good student café: comfortable seats, dependable power and prices that make sense between classes. Twenty percent is Japanese car culture, and the last ten percent is motorsport detail you can ignore or obsess over.

The result is a place where the engineering student, the art student and the person who only wanted a latte all find a seat.

The concept at a glance

Coffee, cars and campus vibes in one building. The board shows how the pieces fit: a main café, a study lounge, a JDM display corner and a short menu, with the busy spaces on the ground floor and the quiet ones upstairs.

Our colors are black and violet, with warm amber lights so the café never feels cold.
